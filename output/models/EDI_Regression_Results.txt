------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THE UNADJUSTED SIGNAL APPEARS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The EDI itself tracks rurality. Spearman rho vs RUCC = 0.280, mean EDI 39.9 in metropolitan counties versus 56.9 in nonmetropolitan counties, a gap of 17.0 points.
Because accredited capacity is concentrated in metropolitan counties, an
unadjusted deprivation model absorbs that rurality signal. Once metropolitan
status is in the model the deprivation term is no longer associated with capacity.

* P < 0.05
