PLAIN-LANGUAGE SUMMARY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Our EDI shows a significant unadjusted CMR association (IRR 0.937, P 0.0018).
The validated external SDI does not (IRR 1.006, P 0.746).
So the unadjusted result is specific to our index. After adjusting for
metropolitan status, neither index is associated with capacity, and metro
status carries a large effect with both indices (CMR metro IRR near 8).
Our EDI tracks rurality more strongly than the SDI, which explains why our
index detects the rurality-driven signal while the more urban-balanced SDI
does not. The paper's central point about metropolitan concentration holds
with both indices.
