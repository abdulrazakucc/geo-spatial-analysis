PLAIN-LANGUAGE SUMMARY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Our EDI unadjusted CMR association: IRR 0.948, P 0.0029 (significant).
External SDI, same model: IRR 1.017, P 0.322 (not significant).
So the unadjusted CMR result is specific to our index.

After adjusting for metropolitan status, the index terms that remain
significant at P < 0.05 are:
  SVI CCT: IRR 1.033 (1.009-1.058), P 0.0076
  SDI CMR: IRR 1.035 (1.001-1.070), P 0.0425
  SDI CCT: IRR 1.035 (1.012-1.058), P 0.0027

Metropolitan status carries a large effect with every index (CMR metro IRR 8.3 to 8.7).
Our EDI tracks rurality more strongly than the SDI, which explains why our
index detects the rurality-driven signal while the more urban-balanced SDI
does not. The paper's central point about metropolitan concentration holds
with all three indices.
